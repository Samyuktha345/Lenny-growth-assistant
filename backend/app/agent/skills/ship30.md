# Ship 30 for 30 Essay Guidelines

A framework for writing atomic, high-signal, practitioner-grade essays based on Lenny's Podcast transcripts.

## 1. The Hook
- **Single-Sentence Opener**: Start with a bold, counter-intuitive statement or friction-free hook.
- **The Problem / Paradox**: Highlight a common trap, broken conventional wisdom, or high-stakes challenge product and growth leaders face.
- **The Promise**: Clarify in 1-2 sentences exactly what tactical framework or insight the reader will gain.

## 2. Structure & Cadence
- **Rhythmic Cadence (1-3-1)**: Alternate between single punchy sentences and compact 2-3 sentence paragraphs. Avoid dense walls of text.
- **Logical Flow**:
  1. *The Hook & Stakes* (Why standard playbook fails).
  2. *The Core Framework* (Deconstruct into 3-4 distinct pillars or phases).
  3. *Practitioner Playbook* (Concrete steps, guest examples, and tactical implementation).
  4. *The 1 Key Takeaway* (The actionable bottom line).
  5. *Sources* (Formal citations).

## 3. Formatting & Skimmability
- **Informative Subheadings**: Use clear, compelling H2 and H3 headers that tell a story on their own.
- **Selective Bolding**: Bold key phrases, metrics, and core rules so a reader skimming in 30 seconds absorbs 80% of the value.
- **Bullet Points & Numbered Lists**: Use lists whenever breaking down frameworks, steps, rules of thumb, or checklists.
- **Whitespace**: Keep paragraphs strictly under 3 sentences.

## 4. Tone & Voice
- **Direct & High-Conviction**: Write like a seasoned operator sharing proven playbooks from the trenches.
- **Zero Fluff**: Eliminate throat-clearing, corporate jargon, and filler adjectives. Every sentence must carry insight.
- **Empirical & Grounded**: Support every claim with concrete wisdom, frameworks, and quotes from transcript guests.

## 5. The Takeaway
- Conclude with a dedicated section: `## The 1 Key Takeaway` or `## The Bottom Line`.
- Provide a memorable rule of thumb or an immediate action the reader can implement right away.

## 6. Citations & Sources
- **Inline Citations**: Every substantive framework, statistic, or quote must cite its origin using `[Guest, Episode]`.
- **Sources Section**: Conclude the essay with a formal `## Sources` section listing the guests, episode titles, and source files referenced.

## 7. Word Count Target
- Target length: **~1,250 words** (acceptable range: **1,100 to 1,400 words**).
